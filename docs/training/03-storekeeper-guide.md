# Storekeeper guide

You receive goods, move stock between stores and count it. Everything is in the back office (web browser) under **Stock** and **Purchasing**.

## Receiving a delivery

1. **Purchasing → Goods received → New**, choose the purchase order (or the supplier when there is none).
2. Enter what **actually arrived** — count it, do not copy the delivery note. Batch numbers and expiry dates where the product asks for them.
3. **Post**. The stock goes up and the cost is recorded. A posted receipt cannot be changed: a mistake is corrected by reversing it and receiving again.

**Why:** the receipt is what the supplier is paid against, and what the shelves are measured against.

## Moving stock

**Stock → Transfers → New**: from which store, to which store, the items. The sending store **dispatches**, the receiving store **receives** what really arrived. Differences show on the transfer.

## Counting

1. **Stock → Stock counts → New** for the store (all products, or one category). Print the **count sheet**.
2. Count what is on the shelf. Do **not** look at the system's figure while counting.
3. Type the counts in (or import the sheet: **Import counts**). **Submit** for approval.
4. A manager approves; the differences become stock movements with their value.

## Adjusting stock (damage, expiry, theft)

**Stock → Stock adjustments → New**, reason, items, quantities. Above the limit, another manager approves. Never adjust to "make the count right" without a count.

## Practise first

In the **practice company**: receive a purchase order with one item short, transfer 5 cartons between stores, and count one category with two differences.
