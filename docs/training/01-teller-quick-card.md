# Teller quick card

Keep this card at the till. Practise first in the **practice company** (a purple **PRACTICE** bar shows at the top): nothing there is real.

## Start of the day

1. **Sign in** — your username and PIN. Five wrong PINs lock you out for 15 minutes; a supervisor can reset it.
2. **Open shift** — count the cash in the drawer and type it in as the opening float.
   **With vaults** (your shop uses a Cashier): take the tray the Cashier gives you, count the float in it, choose your vault on the till, tick *I have counted the float* and open. If the float is wrong, do not open: give the tray back.

## Selling

| To… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Add an item | Scan the barcode, or type part of the name and press **Enter** |
| Check a price | Type part of the name: the list shows each item's price (and each pack size's) without adding it |
| Search again | **F2** |
| Sell a carton | Scan the carton barcode, or tap **+ CTN** |
| Change the quantity | Type it in **Qty**, press **Enter** |
| Weigh (loose goods) | Put it on the scale, tap **Weigh** |
| Remove a line | **✕** at the end of the line |
| Wholesale price | Change **Price list** at the top (only if allowed) |
| Customer on account / loyalty | **Customer** → search name or phone |
| Pay | **F12** or **Pay** |

A **red line** has no price on that list: it cannot be sold on it.

Where the shop has switched it on, each item shows **how many are left** (e.g. *14 TAB left*). **None left** in red: the system shows no more in stock; tell a supervisor.

## Details, comment and calculator

- **Details** (next to Clear): the walk-in's **name and phone** (no customer record needed), a **comment** (tick *Print the comment on the receipt* if the customer should see it), and in a pharmacy the patient's **age, sex, weight** and pregnancy. Some branches ask for a name or phone before payment: the till tells you.
- **Calculator (F1)**: tap or type a sum (`200 + 10%` = 220). On the payment screen, **Calculator → Use as amount** puts the result in *Cash received*.
- **Change:** the payment screen shows the change in notes and coins (e.g. 1 × GH₵20 · 1 × GH₵5).

## Taking payment

- **Cash** — tap the amount handed over (or type it). The change shows and prints.
- **Mobile money** — choose the network, enter the **transaction ID** from the customer's confirmation message. Accept only after you see the confirmation.
- **Card** — run the card on the terminal first, then enter the approval code from the slip.
- **Mixed** — e.g. part MoMo, rest cash. The sale completes when nothing is left to pay.

Then **Print receipt** (prints straight away on the desktop till's receipt printer), or **Show print preview** to look at it first, or **Send receipt** by SMS/WhatsApp/e-mail. Then **New sale**.

## Returns and mistakes

- **Return** — scan or type the receipt number, choose the items, give the reason. Without a receipt you need a supervisor.
- **Discount above your limit** — a supervisor approves with their username and PIN.
- **Wrong item rung up** — remove the line before paying. After payment it is a return: never ring the sale up again.

## The drawer (button **Drawer** at the top)

- **Pay out (expense)** — money taken from the drawer for the shop (taxi, water, cleaning…): the amount, what it was **spent on**, who was paid, what for, and a photo of the receipt if you have one. Keep the paper receipt with the cash. Above the limit (GHS 100 unless your manager changed it) a supervisor types their username and PIN.
- **Pay in** — cash added to the drawer (e.g. more change from the safe), with the reason.
- **Cash drop** — when the yellow bar says the drawer holds too much cash, a supervisor counts some out in front of you and takes it to the safe; they type their PIN.
- **Open drawer (no sale)** — only with a reason (e.g. change for a customer). Every opening is recorded under your name.

Each of these changes the cash expected when you close, so the drawer still balances. After a pay-out, pay-in or cash drop a **slip** prints: both of you sign it. For a cash drop, press **Print another copy**: one slip goes in the safe bag, one stays with the drawer. Clip a pay-out slip to the shop's receipt.

## Expiry dates

Medicines and other batch items show the expiry of the pack you should sell next (*Exp 06/2027*), in the search list and on the sale. **Amber** = expires within 3 months; **red** = expired — do not sell it, tell your supervisor.

## When the network or server is down

The bottom right says **"Server not answering — working offline"** or **"No network"**. **Keep selling.** Sales are kept on the till and sent on their own when the server is back. A yellow bar counts what is waiting. **Never ring a sale up twice.** A red bar means the server refused something: call your supervisor.

## End of the shift

**Close shift** → count **all** the cash in the drawer (float included), note by note and coin by coin. Under **Mobile money, card, vouchers and coupons**, type what the MoMo merchant phone shows for your shift for each network (MTN, Telecel, AT), the card terminal's total, the value of the gift vouchers and credit notes you took, and how many paper coupons you collected; leave a box empty if you do not take it → **Close shift**. Only then do you see over/short, for the cash and for each of these. Add a note if there is a difference. Sign out.

**With vaults:** you do not see the expected figure. Close the tray and hand it to the Cashier, who counts it again.

## Something wrong?

Tap **Report a problem** (bottom of the screen): say what you did and what happened. Support gets it with a picture of your screen (untick *Include a picture of the screen* if it shows something private). Then tell your supervisor.
