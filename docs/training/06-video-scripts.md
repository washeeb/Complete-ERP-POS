# Training video scripts

Short screen recordings (2–4 minutes each) to record yourself, in the **practice company** so no real data shows. Record at 1280×720, the till full-screen; speak slowly; one task per video. Each script lists what to show and what to say.

## 1. Your first sale (cashiers, 3 min)

| Show | Say |
|---|---|
| The sign-in screen | "Type your username, then your PIN. Never tell anyone your PIN." |
| Open shift, type 200 | "Count the cash in the drawer. Type exactly what you counted." |
| Scan two items | "Scan each item. The line shows the name, the quantity and the price." |
| Type "milo", Enter | "No barcode? Type part of the name and press Enter." |
| Change Qty to 3 | "To sell three, type 3 in Qty and press Enter." |
| F12, cash 100 | "Press F12 to pay. Tap or type what the customer gave you. The change is on the screen." |
| Print receipt, New sale | "Give the receipt and the change together. New sale starts the next customer." |

## 2. Mobile money and mixed payment (cashiers, 2 min)

Show choosing MTN MoMo, typing the transaction ID from the customer's phone, then paying the rest in cash. Say: "Only accept once you see the confirmation message on the customer's phone. Copy the transaction ID exactly."

## 3. When the network is down (cashiers, 2 min)

Unplug the network cable on camera. Show the status changing to *No network*, a sale still completing, the yellow bar counting waiting sales, then plug back in and the bar clearing. Say: "Keep selling. Never ring a sale up twice. It sends by itself."

## 4. Returns (cashiers and supervisors, 3 min)

Show a return with the receipt number, choosing one item and the reason; then a return without a receipt with the supervisor approval box. Say: "A return is never done by selling a negative item: always use Return."

## 5. Closing the shift (cashiers, 2 min)

Show Close shift, counting, typing the count, the over/short shown afterwards, adding a note. Say: "Count everything in the drawer, including the float. You see the difference only after you type your count."

## 6. Receiving a delivery (storekeepers, 3 min)

Show a goods receipt against a purchase order with one item short and an expiry date. Say: "Type what arrived, not what the paper says."

## 7. The stock count (storekeepers, 4 min)

Show creating a count, printing the sheet, typing counts, submitting, and the manager approving. Say: "Count without looking at the system's figure."

## 8. Reading your business (owners and managers, 4 min)

Show the dashboard, Insights → Forecast and Alerts, and the report builder making "sales by product this month". Say: "Ten minutes every morning is enough."

## 9. Going live, step by step (owners and managers, 4 min)

Show System → Go-live: the plan, the checklist turning green, the figures check, entering a parallel-run day, taking the go-live backup. Say: "Do not go live with a red step you cannot explain."
