## IT and support activity, and the list filter bug (2026-11-15)
- Owner's choice (2026-11-13): "Owner sees all IT activity". Step 5 of keeping the owner on the business and IT on the technical side: the owner cannot change the technical side, so they must be able to see it. One read-only page, built on the audit log (which cannot be changed), rather than a new log: nothing new to keep in step, and every row already has who, when, what, the computer and the reason.
- What counts: **everything** IT Admin and support did (even business screens, should they reach one), plus **technical changes by anyone** (users, access, access requests, roles, devices, computers, settings, sync, remote sessions, API keys, modules, backups, licence, sign-in security such as two-step and passkeys), so a change made by someone else with a technical role, or the owner, is visible too. Left out: a person's own dashboard and favourites (not a change to the system), and everyday trading (it is on its own screens). "Technical people" includes anyone who *ever* held a technical role, so taking the role away does not hide what they did.
- Who sees it: the owner, support, and anyone allowed to read the audit log. Not staff.
- Found while testing: Filament passes a filter's query by the **parameter name** `$query`. Thirteen filters used `$q` and got an empty query: they silently did nothing, and Customers → *Owes money* crashed the list. **Why a scan test, not one test per filter:** the mistake is in the name, so a test that reads every Filament file stops it everywhere, including filters not written yet.
