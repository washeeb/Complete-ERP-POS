## Favourite tiles per branch
- **Who sets them: whoever may change the branch's till settings (`pos.settings`).** A branch manager has it for their branch; the owner everywhere. Choosing what sells fastest at a shop is a shop decision, not company setup (Company → Branches is for setup rights only), so it is its own page under Sales.
- **A column of its own, not `branches.settings`:** that field holds only branch overrides of company settings and is rewritten whole on each branch save.
- **Order is the owner's:** the till shows them exactly in the order set; products no longer sold there drop out by themselves.
- **48 at most:** one screen of tiles; more belongs in categories.
- **Found while testing:** a short form's save bar floated half-way up; pages are now a column and the bar takes the space above it.
