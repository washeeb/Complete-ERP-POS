## Customers in the new back office
- **Same services, same rules.** Saving goes through `CustomerService::save`, credit through `setCredit`, the opening balance through `openingBalance`, payments through `ReceivablesService::recordPayment`, patient details through `PharmacyService::savePatient`, follow-ups through `Crm::logActivity`, archive and restore through `ArchiveService`. The screens only gather what the person types and show what the services allow.
- **Credit stays out of the edit form**, as on the current screen: it is set when adding at head office, and afterwards only with **Credit** and a reason.
- **Patient details were refused to branch pharmacists.** Found while testing: `savePatient` asked for `pharmacy.patients` company-wide, while the button showed for the permission held anywhere. A pharmacist given the role at one branch saw the button and was refused, on the current screen too. Customers are shared by every branch, so the service now accepts the permission held at any branch, the same rule as the button and the till's health checks.
- **The list's search also matches the contact person,** and the phone written the stored way (+233…) when typed as 024…, as the current global search does.
- **Statement, Follow-up and New deal:** the statement opens the existing print page with the chosen dates; New deal opens the current CRM form, which is not rebuilt yet.
- **Save on the form waits for the industry** when the person works in more than one, as the current form requires it. Otherwise the service would file the customer under the home branch's industry, or none.
