## Batches & expiry in the new back office
- **Same queries and services as the current page,** so the suggestions, markdown prices and corrections cannot differ between the two while both exist.
- **Add or move a batch stays a draft adjustment** opened next for checking and posting, so the usual approval above the limit still applies.
- **The store is checked on every action,** not only on the page: a changed request cannot reach another branch's batches.
- **The "all batches" list keeps the current limit of 500** (earliest expiry first) and says so when it is reached.
