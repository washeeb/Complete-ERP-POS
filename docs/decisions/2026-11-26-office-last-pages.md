## Departments, Tax and SSNIT rules and the setup wizard in the new back office
- **My profile is a part of its own (39):** it holds two-factor sign-in (the authenticator app set-up and recovery codes) and passkeys, security flows that need their own care and checks; the other three pages are plain forms.
- **The wizard's brand is sent with the form** (the logo and icon as files) and saved after the company, exactly as the current wizard: a logo problem is reported and never undoes the setup.
- **Not set up yet opens the wizard first,** as the current back office's redirect, but only for someone who may set the company up; the licence page stays reachable so a licence can be activated first.
- **The "page inside" check moved to My profile,** the last current page reached from the menus.
