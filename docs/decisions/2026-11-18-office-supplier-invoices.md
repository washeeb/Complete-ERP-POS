## Invoices from suppliers and consignment to pay in the new back office
- **Same services, same rules.** Mark entered and Turn back through `SupplierPortal`, Read with AI through `InvoiceScanner::scan`, Settle through `ConsignmentService::settle`.
- **Entering the bill stays on the current form.** Enter as bill and Read with AI open the current supplier bill form (Finance, rebuilt later), as the current buttons do.
- **No page per invoice.** The current screen has none; a row opens a dialog with its facts and buttons, which keeps the list the place to work from.
- **Export on Invoices from suppliers**, as on every current list; Consignment to pay is a small table on the current screen too, without one.
