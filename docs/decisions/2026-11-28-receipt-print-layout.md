## Desktop till receipts follow the screen receipt
- **Owner 2026-11-28: the screen receipt's format is preferred.** The silent printout now mirrors it part for part, rather than printing the screen receipt as a picture: printer text stays fast, sharp and small on thermal printers, and the screen receipt is itself a monospaced 80 mm layout, so it maps to columns one-to-one.
- **One character spare on every line,** for printers and drivers that start a new line when a line is completely full (the cause of the amounts printing below their labels). It costs nothing on printers that do not.
- **Words are never broken:** long names and lines wrap between words, in their column for items.
- **Not caused by the till new look work:** the desktop print layout predates it and was not part of the restyle.
