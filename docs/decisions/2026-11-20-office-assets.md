## Fixed assets in the new back office
- **Registering still does not post the cost,** as now: the purchase is a supplier bill (or the opening balances), so the cost is never in the books twice. The form says so.
- **Disposing asks where the money went only when money was received,** and the server refuses proceeds without an account, so the gain or loss always has its other side.
- **Kinds of asset sit with the register** (a button on it), not as a menu item of their own, as on the current screen.
