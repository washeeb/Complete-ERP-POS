## Confirm it's you before money leaves (2026-11-15)
- Owner's choice (2026-11-13): "Owner re-confirms big money", limit GH₵ 3,000 (2026-11-15). Applied to whoever does it (step-up authentication, as banks do), not only the owner: the risk is a session left open or stolen, whoever's it is.
- Above the limit: supplier payments made at the screen, expense claims paid, payroll paid, money moved between accounts (back office), staff loans paid out, credit notes for returned goods. Always: credit notes with no goods back (a debt written off), new bank or MoMo details for a supplier or a member of staff (payments could be redirected), and changing the limit (a stolen session must not raise it first). **Not:** a branch banking its takings (money into the bank), a payment recorded by an approved direct purchase, the API, the server, the tills (PINs and their own approvals).
- Enforced in the domain services, for people at a screen (actor source web), with a session time window of 5 minutes, so it cannot be skipped by another screen. The note opens the confirmation in a new tab so the form keeps what was typed; then the person presses again. Ways to confirm: authenticator code, passkey, a code by WhatsApp or e-mail (SignInCode), or the password for someone with none of these. **Why not a code field on every form:** one page covers every money screen and every way to confirm, including passkeys.
