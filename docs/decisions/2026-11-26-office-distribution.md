## Sales & distribution in the new back office
- **The industry pages are split into three parts** (Distribution, Manufacturing, Fashion with Electronics): fifteen pages in one part would be too much to check.
- **A settlement is approved by someone other than who submitted it,** as the current rule; the storekeeper who counts and submits gets a refusal, and a manager settles.
- **Route stops are reordered with up and down buttons** instead of dragging, so it works on a phone and with the keyboard.
- **A van's own page stays the list's dialog:** the current page has only its form (no history), so a dialog is enough; its old edit address opens the vans list.
- **Verified with the module on:** the dev company has Distribution off, so check:office was run once with it switched on for HQ, then off again.
- **Distribution stays under More,** as in the current menu (it is not one of the named tabs); office-check looks for it there.
