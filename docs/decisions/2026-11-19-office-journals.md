## Journals in the new back office
- **Posted journals stay immutable:** the page offers Reverse (journals typed by hand and opening balances), never edit; automatic journals are corrected at their source document, as now.
- **The form checks the balance as you type** (debits against credits) and only sends filled lines; the journal service still refuses an unbalanced journal, so the rule holds whatever sends it.
- **Control accounts are not offered** on a journal (as now): customers, suppliers and stock are posted by their own screens so the sub-ledgers stay tied to the ledger.
