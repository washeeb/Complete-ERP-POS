## Till new look, part 2: payment and receipt
- **The amount due and the change are the largest things on screen:** they are what the cashier collects and hands back; mistakes there cost money.
- **One main action per screen:** on the receipt screen it is New sale (focused, so Enter starts the next customer); printing is secondary because the desktop till prints by itself.
- **The receipt keeps its printed form** on screen (left): what the customer gets is what the cashier sees; the new look only frames it.
- **Every control kept its name** (the ways to pay stay a tab list with the same names), so the existing tests and screen readers are unaffected.
