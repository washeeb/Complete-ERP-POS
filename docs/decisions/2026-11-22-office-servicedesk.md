## Service desk in the new back office
- **New ticket is a page, not a dialog,** like the current form: it has three parts (the ticket, who it is for, where and who) and enough fields that a dialog would scroll.
- **Someone who may only ask for help is held to it on the server,** not only in the form: whatever they send is opened as their own staff request, with nobody assigned, as the current rule in ServiceDesk::open.
- **Work order still opens the current maintenance page** (Maintenance is not rebuilt yet); the address follows it inside the new back office.
- **The conversation keeps its three looks:** theirs on the left, ours on the right, staff notes across the full width.
