## The Cash area in the new back office
- **The handover's "count again" is remembered by the server** (in the person's sign-in), not sent by the page: the first blind count that differs from the cashier's asks for a recount, and only the second stands. The current page kept it in page state, which a person could change; the till vault's recount was already stored on the record.
- **Income control lists the branches of "view cash" and "income control" together.** The current page used the "view cash" branches and fell back to the other only when the first was missing, so someone with income control at a branch they could not view saw nothing there. Every role in the catalogue has both, so no install changes today.
- **Records are looked up within the person's branches** before the service runs (a vault, tray, handover or difference elsewhere is "not found"); a teller's own vault can be changed only within the branches the person sets up, as moving one in from another branch was not checked by the service.
- **One blind-count dialog** (`BlindCount`) for the manager's vault count, the cashier's tray and the income controller's bundle, so the three cannot drift apart.
