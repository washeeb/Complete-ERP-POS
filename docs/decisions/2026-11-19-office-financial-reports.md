## Financial reports in the new back office
- **One page, eight reports, as now**, with the same filters, so the accountant's way of working does not change.
- **The server writes the cells** (amounts formatted, links, red/green) and the page draws them as they come. The screen and the export then cannot disagree, and the report logic stays in one place (`FinancialReports`), as the classic page's "export what is shown" did.
- **Links stay inside the reports:** an account opens its ledger with the same dates; a journal opens the classic journal page inside the new back office until journals are rebuilt.
