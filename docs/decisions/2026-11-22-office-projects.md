## Projects in the new back office
- **Projects first, Service desk next:** the two were planned as one part; Projects alone is a full part (a project page with tasks, milestones, costs and stock), so the Service desk is part 28.
- **"Invoice the selected" became ticking costs on the project page:** the same rule (only costs not yet invoiced can be ticked), with the button showing how many are ticked.
- **Time to approve keeps the grouping by person** as one panel per person, each with its total, and approve / send back works on one entry or on everything ticked across people.
- **Every action goes through the Projects and Timesheets services**, so the rules (closed projects, 24 hours a day, milestones not above the agreed price, own time approved by someone else) are the same as on the current pages.
