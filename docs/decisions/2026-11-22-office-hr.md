## HR & payroll in the new back office
- **Pay and bank details only reach HR**: the employee form (and its data) opens only for people who manage HR, as the current page; others see the list.
- **A payslip's name and pay method come from its snapshot**, taken when the payroll was worked out, so a later change to the employee does not rewrite an old payroll.
- **Bank details stay when the pay method changes**, as on the current form (the hidden fields are not cleared).
- **Leave requests go up as a form** so the doctor's note can go with them; a person may only cancel their own request from My leave & payslips.
