## New back office: brand colours readable in light and dark (branch `office-contrast`, base `main`)
- **`pos/office/src/theme.ts`:** `themes(hex)` = Fluent's light/dark themes from the brand ramp, then `brandText` (every brand text token: Foreground1/2, Link, CompoundBrandForeground, Neutral*Brand*) set to the nearest ramp shade reaching `MIN_CONTRAST` 4.5:1 on `colorNeutralBackground1` (lighter in dark, darker in light; white/black if none), and `brandFill` (BrandBackground, CompoundBrandBackground/Stroke, BrandStroke1) set to the first shade reaching `MIN_CONTROL_CONTRAST` 3:1 against the page on which one text colour (`textOn`: white or black) reads 4.5:1 on the normal, hover and pressed shades. `contrast()` is WCAG's ratio.
- **`theme.test.ts`:** 13 brand colours (incl. navy, black, white, yellow, lime, pink) × light/dark: text tokens ≥ 4.5 on backgrounds 1–3; filled controls ≥ 3 and their text ≥ 4.5. The old theme failed all 13 in dark (navy links 2.9:1).
