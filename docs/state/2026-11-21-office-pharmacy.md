## New back office, part 11: pharmacy records (branch `office-pharmacy`, base `main`)
- **Server:** `App\Domain\Office\PharmacyScreen` (prescriptions list and detail, refill reminders, interactions, controlled-drug register from `PharmacyReports::controlledRegister`) and `Office\PharmacyController` (through `RefillReminders::sendNow/cancel`, `PharmacyService::saveInteraction/addStarter`). Who may see and do what is the classic screens' (`PrescriptionResource`, `RefillReminderResource`, `DrugInteractionResource`, `ControlledDrugRegister`); Send now and Cancel need `customers.manage` in the reminder's branch, as before.
- **Routes:** `office-api/prescriptions`, `prescriptions/{id}`, `refill-reminders`, POST `refill-reminders/{id}/{send|cancel}`, `drug-interactions`, POST `drug-interactions`, `drug-interactions/starter`, `drug-interactions/{id}`, `controlled-drug-register`.
- **Client:** `pages/PrescriptionsPage.tsx` (a row opens the prescription in a dialog, `?open=id`), `RefillRemindersPage.tsx`, `DrugInteractionsPage.tsx`, `ControlledRegisterPage.tsx`; `/admin/prescriptions`, `/admin/prescriptions/{uuid}` (→ `?open=`), `/admin/refill-reminders`, `/admin/drug-interactions`, `/admin/controlled-drug-register` map to them.
- **Tests:** `OfficePharmacyTest`. office-check opens the four screens from Industries → Pharmacy where the module is on (the demo company has it on Branch C).
- **Next:** pharmacy part 12 (NHIS claims, medicine sign-offs, temperature log, pharmacy markdowns, expiry returns).
