## New back office, part 41: Signing in (branch `office-signin`, base `office-confirm`)
- **Server:** `Office\SignInController` (`office-auth/state|sign-in|verify|choose|cancel`, open signed out, 30 a minute; `office-api/sign-out`): the current sign-in step for step (Filament `Login` + `App\Filament\Auth\Login`): e-mail or username; five tries a minute per address; `Timebox` around every failure; `canAccessPanel` before the challenge; the challenge kept in the session for 10 minutes; `MultiFactorChallenge` providers (app code with one-use codes, recovery codes, `SignInCode` sent before the challenge, choose / resend / by e-mail); its own rate limit; the password hash and access checked again before `Auth::login` (events: audit, new computer, support notice); session regenerated; back to the page asked for (only `/office/…`) or the person's home.
