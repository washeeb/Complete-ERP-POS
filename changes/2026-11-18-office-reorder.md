### Added
- **Reorder levels, What to order and Suggested levels rebuilt in the new back office (release 5, part 6).** Purchasing is now complete in the new back office.
  - **Reorder levels:** own figures and smart levels per store in the buying unit, with what is on hand; Own figure for a product (one store or every store), Change, Use the default; Export.
  - **What to order:** what is at or below its minimum, with supplier, unit, quantity, cost and stock another store can spare; change a line (kept until ordering, with Undo) or its levels; Ask other stores to send; Create draft purchase orders, one per supplier.
  - **Suggested levels:** levels worked out from sales, to accept; Work them out now.

### Fixed
- **Reorder levels → Own figure** no longer fails for a product bought in packs. Quantities stored with six decimals (unit sizes such as 24.000000) are now shown as "24" everywhere instead of stopping the page.
