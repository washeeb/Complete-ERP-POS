### Added
- **A till added in place of a wiped one continues the shift left open on it** (owner, 2026-11-28). After sign-in it shows *A shift was left open on this till* (who, since when, how many sales); a manager approves by username and PIN, and the shift (float and sales) carries on and is closed on the new till. Offered once the old till is revoked or silent for 15 minutes; what the old till still sends lands in the same shift. Or *Open a new shift instead*.
- **Devices:** a device with an open shift shows it (with a link), and revoking it reminds you the shift stays open and how to close it.

### Fixed
- **A shift on a cash-control vault can be closed from the back office** (it could not be closed anywhere once its till was gone). The count is blind; the manager's count stands for the teller's and the cashier receives the vault as usual.
