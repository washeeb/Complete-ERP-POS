### Added
- **Products rebuilt in the new back office (release 3 begins).** **Stock → Products** now opens the new screen.
  - **The list:** SKU, name (with the medicine schedule and the shelf), category, industry, the standard price, the stock on hand in the branches you may see, and whether it is for sale.
  - **Finding products:** search by SKU, name or shelf, or scan a barcode into the search box; filters for industry, category, for sale, medicine schedule and out of stock. The cards show how many products there are, how many are for sale, and how many are out of stock.
  - **Actions on ticked products** (or on every product the search and filters pick): print labels, export prices, change category/brand/tax, change the unit, switch on batches and expiry, set the medicine schedule, move to an industry, work out cost from selling price. Products that cannot be changed are listed with the reason.
  - **One product on its own page:** details, medicine, units and barcodes, prices (with the pricing rule and a below-cost warning), stock in each warehouse, batches with expiry dates, and the latest stock movements; **Print labels** and **Change the unit** from there.
  - **New product, Edit and the file imports** still open the current form inside the new back office for now.
