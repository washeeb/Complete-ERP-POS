### Added
- **Stock counts rebuilt in the new back office (release 3, part 4).**
  - **The list:** status, warehouse and period filters; cards for counts being counted and waiting for approval; the difference value once posted; Export.
  - **Start a count** for a warehouse or one bin, blind or not.
  - **On a count:**
    - **Enter counts** takes several products at once, each in any of its units, and warns when a product is on two lines (a recount replaces the earlier count).
    - The count sheet (PDF or CSV) and the CSV import; Submit, Approve and post by someone else, and Cancel.
  - A blind count keeps the expected figures hidden from counters until it is submitted.
