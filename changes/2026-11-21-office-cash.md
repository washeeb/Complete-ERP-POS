### Added
- **The Cash area rebuilt in the new back office (part 14).** Close the day: whether the day is wrapped up (tills closed, cash counted and handed over, sales received, approvals, backup…), then close it with the cash taken to the bank (and its slip) or kept in the safe; close it again after a fix; the recent closes. Cash desk: issue each vault's float to its teller, take it back, receive each counted tray with a blind count, hand the trays over. Income control: count each handover blind, decide each difference (cash over/short or charged to the staff member), the reconciliation per vault, record the deposit. Shifts and vaults: the shifts, each till's vault per shift and its standard float, or each teller's own vault and lending it for today.
