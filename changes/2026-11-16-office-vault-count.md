### Added
- **Count the vault in the new back office.** On a shift *waiting for the manager's count* (each teller's own vault, with a manager recount), **Count the vault** now opens a form in the new back office instead of the classic screen.
  - The manager types how many of each note and coin are in the tray. The total adds up as they type; the teller's figure is never shown.
  - If the count differs from the teller's, the boxes clear and the manager counts once more; that second count is booked, with the over or short worked out from it. The same rules as the classic screen.
