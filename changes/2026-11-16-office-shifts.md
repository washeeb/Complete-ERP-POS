### Added
- **Till shifts rebuilt in the new back office.** **Sales → Till shifts** now opens the new screen.
  - **The list:** each shift with its till, who opened it (and who else sold in it), opened and closed, sales and their total, the float, the cash expected, counted (a corrected count shows the corrected figure) and over or short, and the MoMo, card and voucher checks.
  - **Warnings:** a shift left open overnight is marked in red.
  - **Finding shifts:** Today, 7, 30 or 90 days, or All; **Open now**; filters for open or closed, branch, teller, over or short, dates, and vaults waiting for the manager's count. The cards show how many shifts, how many are still open, and the over or short together.
  - **One shift:** a row opens it with the shift report's own figures and tables (sales by payment method, the cash drawer, the MoMo/card checks, the drawer, coupons).
  - **Actions:** **Shift report** (PDF), **Correct the count**, and **Close shift** for a shift no till can close. Counting a vault stays on the classic screen for now.
- **Till drawer rebuilt.** **Sales → Till drawer** lists the expenses paid out (with the receipt photo), pay-ins, cash drops, drawers opened without a sale and cash-limit warnings, with who did it and who approved it. Cards show the totals of each kind; a row opens its shift.
