### Added
- **Stock requests rebuilt in the new back office (release 3, part 5).** Release 3 (products and stock) is now complete.
  - **The list:** status, "for" and "asked of" warehouse filters, plus **Waiting on you** and **Between industries**; cards for open requests, those waiting on you, and those sent but not yet received; Export.
  - **Ask for stock:** each line shows what you have and what the warehouse asked has; it warns when a product is on two lines, or when you ask for more than they hold.
  - **On a request:**
    - Says whose turn it is.
    - **Approve** (between industries: the asking side, then the supplier).
    - **Send:** what can be spared per line; between industries, your own product for each line. It creates and dispatches the transfer, which the request links to.
    - **Reject** and **Cancel request**, each with a reason, and **Print**.
