### Added
- **Till sales rebuilt in the new back office** (release 2 begins). **Sales → Till sales** now opens the new screen:
  - **Search:** by receipt, teller or walk-in customer.
  - **When:** Today, 7, 30 or 90 days, or All.
  - **Filters:** branch, teller, how it was paid (several at once), amount, dates, sales or returns only. Each filter in use shows as a chip that takes it off.
  - **Totals:** the count, the total and the two biggest ways of paying, for what is shown.
  - **Sorting and pages:** sort by any heading; choose rows per page.
  - **One sale:** a row opens it in full, with Print preview, Print copy and Save as customer for a walk-in.
  - The search and filters stay in the address, so Back and bookmarks work. **Classic version** opens the old screen.
### Fixed
- **"Too many changes in a minute" on the favourite star** while using the new back office. Every request limit on the site shared one counter per person (or per address), so the new back office's own calls used up the star's allowance. For the same reason, adding many items to an online cart counted against checking out. Each limit now counts on its own.
