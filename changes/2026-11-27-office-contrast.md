### Fixed
- **Words in the company's colour are readable in dark and light,** whatever colour the company chose: reminders, links, figures and labels were grey-blue on dark grey with a dark brand colour (and pale on white with a pale one). They now always reach the WCAG AA contrast for text (4.5:1).
- **Buttons filled with the company's colour stand out** (Create, Save, selected items): with a dark brand colour in dark mode they almost disappeared. They now reach 3:1 against the page, with white or black text on them, whichever reads.
