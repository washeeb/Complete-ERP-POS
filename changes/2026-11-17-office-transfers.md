### Added
- **Transfers rebuilt in the new back office (release 3, part 4).**
  - **The list:** status, from and to warehouse, and "differences only" filters; cards for transfers in transit, yours to receive, and drafts; Export.
  - **The draft form:** each line shows what the sending warehouse has to send and warns when you ask for more, or when a product is on two lines; serial numbers for serialised items.
  - **On a transfer:**
    - **Dispatch**, and **Receive** with what actually arrived per line, the bin and a note. A difference goes to the review queue.
    - **Cancel** a draft, **Print**, and **Make a waybill**.
