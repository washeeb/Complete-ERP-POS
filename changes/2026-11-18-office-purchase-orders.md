### Added
- **Purchase orders rebuilt in the new back office (release 5, part 2).**
  - **The list:** search by number or supplier; filters for status (several), supplier and warehouse; cards for orders waiting for approval and on their way; Export; **Order from a file** (Excel/CSV list or a supplier's pro-forma read by the AI).
  - **An order:** totals, supplier (opens the supplier), store, who made, approved and sent it, the supplier's confirmation, and the lines ordered and received.
  - **Buttons, as on the current page:** Submit for approval, Approve, Send back, Send to supplier, Receive goods (bins, batch and expiry with the pack-code scan, serial numbers), Confirm delivered, Print, Edit; under More: Order again, Close short, Cancel order, Goods received.
  - **The draft form:** supplier (add a missing one), store, date, department, and product or service lines with the supplier's pack rule and the store's stock shown.

### Changed
- **Approve** is no longer offered on an order you made yourself (it was refused anyway: someone else approves it).
