### Added
- **Quotes & orders, Payments received and Aged receivables rebuilt in the new back office (part 15).** Quotes & orders: search and filter by status, total and who entered it; a quote or order on its own page with Turn into order, Release credit hold, Deliver (raises the invoice for what is delivered), Print, Waybill, Invoices and Mark lost / Cancel order; the quotation form with the customer (add a missing one there), the store, how long it holds and the lines with what the store has. Payments received: every payment on account, filtered by method, who received it, amount and dates, with the receipt to print. Aged receivables: what each customer owes by how late. Both lists export.
