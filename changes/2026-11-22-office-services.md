### Added
- **Services rebuilt in the new back office (part 26).** Staff calendar: one day at a branch, a column per staff member who takes appointments, with Book appointment and Arrived, No-show, Change and Cancel on each booking. Appointments: the list (from today by default) with the same actions. Prepaid passes: packs and membership periods with sessions left, their history and Void. Memberships: the list, Sign up a customer (first period invoiced at once), Invoice due periods, Pause, Resume and Cancel. Membership plans: add, change (price, how often, invoiced-as product, sessions included), archive and restore. Commissions: per staff member for a period, with the lines behind it. Licence keys: the list (last characters only), Load keys, Hand out keys still owed, Show key and Void.
