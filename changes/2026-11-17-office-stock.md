### Added
- **Stock on hand and the stock ledger rebuilt in the new back office (release 3, part 3).**
  - **Stock → Stock on hand:**
    - Every product in each warehouse you may see. Search by SKU, name or a scanned barcode; filter by warehouse, category, and in, out or negative stock.
    - Cards count the lines in stock, out of stock and negative.
    - **Stock sheet** (PDF or CSV, blind or not), **Opening stock / adjustment** and **Update stock from CSV** work as before.
  - **Stock → Stock ledger:**
    - Every stock movement, newest first, with who and why.
    - Filter by period, product, kind, warehouse, category, brand, person and dates.
    - A product's page links to its own ledger.
