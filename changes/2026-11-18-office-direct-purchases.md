### Added
- **Direct purchases rebuilt in the new back office (release 5, part 4).**
  - **The list:** search by number, invoice or supplier; filters for status and how it is paid; cards for what is posted, waiting for approval and drafts; Export.
  - **Enter a supplier invoice:** the invoice and its pages, how it is paid (on delivery, on credit, on consignment), and each product with batch, expiry and a new selling price (margin and short-dated batches shown beside it). **Scan the invoice** fills it with the AI; a missing product is added on its line.
  - **A purchase:** what to check before posting, its lines with margins, and the goods received, bill and payment it made. Buttons as now: Post, Send for approval, Correct (while waiting for approval), Cancel, Edit, the invoice pages, Print labels.
