### Changed
- **Home is now the Business Portal, and its Tiles are the Action Centre** (page title, house icon, side menu "Portal", search, My profile, the guide). Searching *home*, *dashboard* or *portal* finds it.
- **The save bar at the foot of every form sits flush with the bottom of the window** (it hung above it with the form showing underneath), edge to edge, on 31 forms; the Settings bar at the top likewise.
- **Unsaved work is not lost by accident:** leaving a form with unsaved changes (the menu, a link, Back) asks *Save and leave*, *Leave without saving* or *Stay*; closing the tab or reloading gets the browser's warning. The save bar says *Unsaved changes* and **Ctrl+S** saves.
- **The menu shows where you are on every page:** pages opened from another page (Departments, Tax and SSNIT rules, Kinds of asset) light their area; the house lights only on the Business Portal.
- **Phones always get the side menu** (the top menu's areas overlapped); its panels and the top row fit the screen.
