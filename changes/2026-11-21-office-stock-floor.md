### Added
- **Returns to suppliers, Waybills, Bins and My day rebuilt in the new back office (part 17).** Returns to suppliers: the list (status, settlement, supplier) and one return with Approve and send, Record the supplier's settlement (credit note, replacement, or a refund with its amount and account), Cancel, Waybill and the Return note. Waybills: the list, New waybill from a delivery, transfer, return or goods received, and one waybill with Dispatch, Mark delivered (with the signed copy or a photo), Cancel and Print. Bins: what each holds, New bin with the next code suggested, Move stock between bins, Archive and Restore. My day: today's cycle count (start it there), what needs doing in a store, and the checks.
