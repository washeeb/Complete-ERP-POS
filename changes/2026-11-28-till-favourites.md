### Added
- **Till favourites (Sales → Till favourites):** choose the products a branch's tills show first, as large tiles on a **Favourites** tab, in the order you set (up to 48): add by name, SKU or barcode, move up or down, take off. A branch manager sets their own branch's (the permission to change till settings). Free.
